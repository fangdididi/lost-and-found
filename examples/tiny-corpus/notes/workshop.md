# Workshop notes

## Booking a service

Standard service takes 2 days. Bring the bike before 10:00 for same-week
slots. We do not service bikes bought online elsewhere during June and July,
because that is peak season.

## Tools

The torque wrench lives in the blue cabinet. Carbon parts need the 5 Nm
setting; never use the impact driver on carbon.

## Loan bikes

Customers whose service takes longer than 3 days get a free loan bike.
Loan bikes must be back before the repaired bike is collected.
