# Returns and refunds

Kestrel Bikes is a small fictional bike shop. This page is our internal
returns policy for staff.

## Who can return

Any customer with an order number can return an unused item.
Custom-built wheels cannot be returned once the spokes are cut.

## Time limits

Unused parts: 45 days from delivery.
Complete bikes: 14 days from delivery, and the bike must have under 20 km
on the odometer or no visible tyre wear.

## Money back

We send the money back to the original card within 6 working days after
the item reaches the workshop. Store credit is instant.
Shipping fees are not paid back unless the item was faulty.

## Faulty items

If a part fails inside the warranty window, see warranty.md instead.
