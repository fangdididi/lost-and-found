# Warranty

Frames: 5 years against cracks at welds.
Forks and suspension: 2 years.
Electrical parts on e-bikes (motor, controller, battery): 2 years, but the
battery is only covered while it holds more than 70% of its rated capacity.

Wear parts are never covered: chains, cassettes, brake pads, tyres, grips.

To start a claim the customer sends photos plus the frame serial number,
which is stamped under the bottom bracket.
